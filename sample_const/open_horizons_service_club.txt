CONSTITUTION OF THE OPEN HORIZONS SERVICE CLUB

ARTICLE I - NAME AND PURPOSE
The name of this organization shall be the Open Horizons Service Club. Our purpose
is to organize community service projects and to build an inclusive community where
every student, no matter when or how they arrived at this university, can contribute
and lead.

ARTICLE II - MEMBERSHIP
Section 1. Membership is open to all currently enrolled students of the university.
There are no prerequisites, auditions, or applications. A student becomes a full
voting member the moment they attend their first meeting and add their name to the
roster.

Section 2. Transfer students, returning students, commuter students, and students who
change majors are explicitly welcomed as full members on the same terms as everyone
else. Time spent at the university, the number of credit hours earned here, and the
year in which a student joined have no bearing on membership status.

Section 3. The club maintains a peer mentorship program. Any new or transfer member
may request a mentor during their first two weeks to help them get oriented quickly.

ARTICLE III - OFFICERS AND ELIGIBILITY
Section 1. The officers shall be a President, a Vice President, a Treasurer, and a
Secretary.

Section 2. Any member in good standing is eligible to run for any officer position.
There is NO minimum number of semesters of membership, NO minimum number of credit
hours, and NO class-standing requirement. A first-semester transfer student is just
as eligible to serve as President as a four-year student.

Section 3. Eligibility for leadership is based solely on willingness to serve and the
support of the membership through an open vote.

ARTICLE IV - ELECTIONS
Section 1. Elections are held at the start of every semester, including the spring
semester, so that students who join mid-year are never required to wait a full year
for an opportunity to lead.

Section 2. Nominations are open to all members, including self-nominations. No prior
board experience, internal referral, or nomination by a sitting officer is required.

Section 3. Recruitment drives are held at the beginning of both the fall and spring
semesters so that newly enrolled and transfer students always have an entry point.

ARTICLE V - MEETINGS
Regular meetings are open to all members and to any student who wishes to attend.
Attendance is encouraged but is never used as a barrier to membership or leadership
eligibility.

ARTICLE VI - AMENDMENTS
This constitution may be amended by a two-thirds vote of members present at any
regular meeting, provided the proposed amendment does not create barriers based on a
student's year of entry, transfer status, or length of enrollment.
